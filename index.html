I am an AI engineer and researcher with an MSc in Information Technology from Bahir Dar University (2022) and a BSc in Information Technology (2019). My research applies machine learning and deep learning to natural language processing, clinical imaging and signal processing in low-resource settings.

My work has been published and presented at international venues including MICCAI, IJCAI, AfricaNLP and Deep Learning Indaba. I welcome research collaborations and academic opportunities in AI for health and language technologies.

Research interests

Low-resource language technologies

Fine-tuning pretrained models such as BERT and RoBERTa for Amharic text complexity detection and simplification.

Clinical imaging

Lightweight mobile deep learning for pneumonia detection from chest X-rays.

RAG and multi-agent workflows

Context-aware models built with retrieval and LangGraph, grounded in responsible AI.
