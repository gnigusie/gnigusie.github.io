About Gebregziabihier Nigusie

I worked on RAG that turns user prompts into SQL queries for report generation, and on archiving African heritage sounds with a fusion model for creative reuse. I fine-tuned pretrained models for low-resource languages such as Amharic, and developed a lightweight mobile model for pneumonia detection from chest X-rays. I presented and published some of my work at MICCAI, IJCAI, AfricaNLP and Deep Learning Indaba. I also worked as a lecturer at Mizan-Tepi University, an Ethiopian higher education institution. I am interested in research and project collaborations, especially in AI for language technologies and computer vision.

Research Interests

Fine-tuning pretrained models for low-resource language technologies, lightweight mobile deep learning for clinical imaging, and context-aware RAG and multi-agent workflows grounded in responsible AI.
